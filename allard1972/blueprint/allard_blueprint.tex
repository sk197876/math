\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Allard's paper~\cite{Allard1972} develops the theory of $\vdim$~dimensional varifolds whose first
variation is a~Radon measure. A~varifold~$V$ in an open set $U \subseteq \R^{\adim}$ is a~Radon
measure on $U \times \pgrass{\adim}{\vdim}$, its weight $\|V\|$ is the image of~$V$ under the
projection onto~$U$, and its first variation is $\delta V(g) = \textint{}{} \uD g(x) \bullet S \ud
V(x,S)$ for smooth compactly supported vector fields~$g$. The rectifiability
theorem~\cite[5.5(1)]{Allard1972}, formalised in~\ref{al:5.5(1)}, asserts that if $\|\delta V\|$
is a~Radon measure, then the restriction of~$V$ to the set where $\udensity^{\vdim}(\|V\|,\cdot) >
0$ is a~rectifiable varifold: at almost every such point the density exists, the approximate
tangent cone of $\|V\|$ is a~$\vdim$~dimensional plane~$T$, and the measure on the Grassmannian
attached to the point is the Dirac measure at~$T$. The compactness theorem for rectifiable
varifolds~\cite[5.6]{Allard1972}, formalised in~\ref{al:5.6}, states that the rectifiable varifolds
with locally bounded weight and first variation and with density bounded below by a~fixed positive
continuous function form a~compact set.

The integral analogue~\cite[6.4]{Allard1972} asserts that the integral varifolds~$V$ in~$U$ with
$(\|V\| + \|\delta V\|)(G_i) \le M_i$ for a~countable open cover $G_1, G_2, \ldots$ of~$U$ form
a~compact set. The isoperimetric inequality~\cite[7.1]{Allard1972} bounds, for nonnegative
$\varphi \in \dspace{U}{\R}$, the integral of~$\varphi$ with respect to~$\|V\|$ over the set where
$\density^{\vdim}(\|V\|,\cdot) \ge 1$ by a~constant depending on $\adim$ and~$\vdim$ times
$(\textint{}{} \varphi \ud \|V\|)^{1/\vdim}$ times $\textint{}{} \varphi \ud \|\delta V\| +
\textint{}{} |S(\grad \varphi(x))| \ud V(x,S)$. The regularity theorem~\cite[8.19]{Allard1972}
asserts that if $\density^{\vdim}(\|V\|,\cdot) \ge 1$ almost everywhere, the weight ratio of~$V$
in a~cylinder over a~plane~$T$ is close to that of a~plane, the tilt excess with respect to~$T$ is
small, and the first variation satisfies an $\Lp{p}$ bound with $p > \vdim$, then near the axis
of the cylinder $\spt \|V\|$ is the graph of a~map of class $1$ whose derivative is H\"older
continuous with exponent $1 - \vdim/p$.

Sections~\ref{sec:prelim}--\ref{sec:rectifiability} of this note treat sections~2--5
of~\cite{Allard1972}; sections~6--8, if present in this version, are written separately. Every
numbered item of Allard with mathematical content receives an entry titled by its locator, with
a~precise statement and a~linear proof in which each assertion is matched with an item of
Federer's book~\cite{Federer1969}, an earlier entry, or an auxiliary lemma titled ``new''. Allard
writes his varifolds in a~smooth submanifold~$M$ of an open set~$U$; we keep this generality in
sections~\ref{sec:prelim}--\ref{sec:first_variation} and take $M = U$ in
section~\ref{sec:rectifiability}, as Allard does. Corrections to Allard's text are recorded in
remarks following the corresponding entries.

The Rocq formalisation of Federer's book covers chapters~1--3, except~1.1.3, the examples
of~2.8.8--9, 2.10.46 and the second paragraph of~3.2.40, and the items~4.1.1--4.1.27. Items
from~4.1.28 on, from sections~4.2--4.5, and from chapter~5 are outside the formalised range; they
are listed in~\ref{rem:beyond}. The entries marked~\MS\ form the shortest path to the
Michael--Simon inequality, described in~\ref{rem:ms_path}.

\begin{remark}
    \label{rem:ms_path}
    The Michael--Simon inequality~\cite[Theorem~2.1]{Michael1973}, formulated for varifolds
    in~$\R^{\adim}$, needs only the case $M = U = \R^{\adim}$ and the following entries: the linear algebra \ref{al:2.2(1)}, \ref{al:2.2(2)}, \ref{al:2.3(1)},
    \ref{new:grass_compact}, \ref{new:projection_formula}, \ref{al:2.4} and~\ref{al:2.4(1)};
    measures and weak convergence \ref{al:2.6(1)}, \ref{al:2.6(2)}, \ref{al:2.6(2)(a)},
    \ref{new:countable_dense} and~\ref{new:weak_metric}; densities and rectifiable sets
    \ref{al:2.8(1)}, \ref{al:2.8(2)}, \ref{al:2.8(4)}, \ref{new:rectifiable_equiv},
    \ref{new:simple_to_projection} and~\ref{al:2.8(4)(a)}; varifolds and their images
    \ref{al:3.1}, \ref{al:3.1:lem}, \ref{al:3.2}, \ref{al:3.2(1)} for $C^1$ diffeomorphisms
    of~$\R^{\adim}$ and \ref{al:3.2(2)}; rectifiable varifolds \ref{al:3.5}, \ref{al:3.5:lem},
    \ref{new:density_tangent}, the ``only if'' part of~\ref{al:3.5(1)}(a) together with the
    measurability of $[x \mapsto \Tan^{\vdim}(\|V\|,x)]$, and~\ref{al:3.5(1)}(b); finally the
    definition~\ref{al:4.2} with~\ref{al:4.2:lem}. The proof of~\ref{al:3.5(1)}(b) given first
    proceeds directly from $V = \textsum{i=1}{\infty} c_i \var{}(E_i)$
    through~\cite[2.8.18, 2.9.9, 2.10.19(4), 3.2.19, 3.2.25]{Federer1969}; it avoids~\ref{al:3.3},
    \ref{al:2.8(5)} and~\cite[3.3.17]{Federer1969}. Submanifolds (\ref{al:2.5}--\ref{al:2.5(1)}),
    covering theorems (\ref{al:2.7(1)}, \ref{al:2.7(2)}), varifold tangents~\ref{al:3.4(1)} and
    sections~\ref{sec:first_variation}--\ref{sec:rectifiability} beyond~\ref{al:4.2:lem} are not
    needed on this path.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
    \label{rem:beyond}
    The following items of Federer's book used or cited in this blueprint lie beyond the
    formalised range.
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \cite[4.5.5, 4.5.6, 4.5.11, 4.5.12]{Federer1969}: exterior normals, the
            Gauss--Green theorem and sets of locally finite perimeter, used in~\ref{al:4.7}
            and~\ref{al:4.8}(a). A lighter route for smooth or polyhedral~$E$ uses the
            divergence theorem~\ref{new:divergence} on each face.
        \item \cite[4.2, 4.2.10]{Federer1969}: integral currents and their isoperimetric
            inequality, used in~\ref{al:4.8}(d) and~\ref{al:5.5(3)}; these remarks are not
            formalised. The homotopy formula~\cite[4.1.9]{Federer1969} and the lemma on Lipschitz
            images~\cite[4.1.25]{Federer1969} used there lie within the formalised range.
        \item \cite[5.4.3]{Federer1969}: the monotonicity formula for minimising currents,
            cited by Allard only as motivation for~\ref{al:5.1(1)}.
        \item \cite[4.5.9(18)]{Federer1969}: credited by Allard in~\ref{rem:7.3}; not used in
            the proof.
        \item \cite[5.2.5]{Federer1969}: regularity of solutions of elliptic systems, cited by
            Allard in~\ref{al:8.15}; replaced by~\ref{new:meanvalue}.
        \item \cite[5.2.6]{Federer1969}: regularity of weak solutions, cited by Allard
            in~\ref{al:8.16} (printed there as 2.5.6); replaced by~\ref{new:weyl}.
        \item \cite[4.1.5]{Federer1969}, the representation of distributions of order zero by
            integration, lies inside the formalised range~4.1.1--4.1.27; so does the constancy
            theorem~\cite[4.1.4]{Federer1969}.
    \end{enumerate}
    In sections~\ref{sec:compactness}--\ref{sec:regularity}, the Gauss--Green
    theorem~\cite[4.5.6, 4.5.12]{Federer1969} enters only through~\ref{al:7.2}, which
    uses~\ref{al:4.7}; the corollary~\ref{al:6.4:cor} and~\ref{al:7.4} avoid it. With the
    replacements above, the compactness theorem, the isoperimetric inequality~\ref{al:7.1} and the
    regularity theorem need no item of Federer's book beyond the formalised range.
\end{remark}
