## Source editions and supporting literature

### Bell2016

Jordan Bell. *Measure theory and Perron–Frobenius operators for continued
fractions*. University of Toronto, 18 April 2016. Expository notes.

### BesicovitchMoran1945

Federer’s label: [BM].

A. S. Besicovitch and P. A. P. Moran. “The measure of product and cylinder
sets.” *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* **20** (1945), 110–120.

### BonnesenFenchel1934

Federer’s label: [BF].

Tommy Bonnesen and Werner Fenchel. *Theorie der konvexen Körper*.
Julius Springer, Berlin, 1934.

### Bourbaki

Federer’s label: [BO].

Nicolas Bourbaki. *Éléments de mathématique*. Actualités scientifiques et
industrielles. Hermann, Paris. Multi-volume series; Federer's bibliography
does not specify a volume or year for this collective reference.

### Caratheodory1927

Federer’s label: [C2].

Constantin Carathéodory. *Vorlesungen über reelle Funktionen*. Second
edition. B. G. Teubner, Leipzig, 1927.

### Cartan1957

Federer’s label: [CH].

Henri Cartan. “Variétés analytiques réelles et variétés analytiques complexes.”
*Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France* **85** (1957), 77–99.
DOI: [10.24033/bsmf.1481](https://doi.org/10.24033/bsmf.1481).

### Cesari1939

Lamberto Cesari. “Sul teorema di densità in senso forte.” *Annali della
Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa, Classe di Scienze*, second series,
**8**, nos. 3–4 (1939), 301–307.
[Publication record](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASNSP_1939_2_8_3-4_301_0/).

### Eggleston1963

Federer’s label: [EG 2].

H. G. Eggleston. *Convexity*. Cambridge University Press, Cambridge, 1963.
Edition cited in Federer's bibliography.

### EilenbergSteenrod1952

Federer’s label: [ES].

Samuel Eilenberg and Norman Steenrod. *Foundations of Algebraic Topology*.
Princeton University Press, Princeton, New Jersey, 1952. xv + 328 pp.

### Federer1947

Federer’s label: [F5].

Herbert Federer. “Dimension and measure.” *Transactions of the American
Mathematical Society* **62** (1947), 536–547.

### Federer1954Abstract

Federer’s label: [F11].

Herbert Federer. “An analytic characterization of distributions whose partial
derivatives are representable by measures.” *Bulletin of the American
Mathematical Society* **60** (1954), 339.

### Federer1955

Federer’s label: [F12].

Herbert Federer. “On Lebesgue area.” *Annals of Mathematics*, second series,
**61** (1955), 289–353. DOI: [10.2307/1969917](https://doi.org/10.2307/1969917).

### Federer1959

Federer’s label: [F15].

Herbert Federer. “Curvature measures.” *Transactions of the American
Mathematical Society* **93**, no. 3 (1959), 418–491.

### Federer1969

Herbert Federer. *Geometric Measure Theory*. Die Grundlehren der
mathematischen Wissenschaften, vol. 153. Springer-Verlag, New York, 1969.
xiv + 676 pp.

### Federer1987Erratum

Herbert Federer. *Geometric Measure Theory: List of corrections*.
May 1987. Cover and 12 numbered pages.

### Freilich1950

Federer’s label: [FR1].

Gerald Freilich. “On the measure of Cartesian product sets.” *Transactions
of the American Mathematical Society* **69** (1950), 232–275.
DOI: [10.2307/1990357](https://doi.org/10.2307/1990357).

### Freilich1965

Federer’s label: [FR2].

Gerald Freilich. “Carathéodory measure of cylinders.” *Transactions of the
American Mathematical Society* **114** (1965), 384–400.
DOI: [10.2307/1994182](https://doi.org/10.2307/1994182).

### Gross1918

Federer’s label: [GR 2].

Wilhelm Gross. “Über das lineare Maß von Punktmengen.” *Monatshefte für
Mathematik und Physik* **29** (1918), 177–193.
DOI: [10.1007/BF01700486](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01700486).

### Kelley2017

John L. Kelley. *General Topology*. Dover Publications, Mineola, New York,
2017. Reprint of the 1955 edition published by D. Van Nostrand.

### Kneser1955

Federer’s label: [KM 1].

*Archiv der Mathematik* **6** (1955), 382–390.
DOI: [10.1007/BF01900510](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01900510).

### Perron1948

Federer’s label: [PO].

Oskar Perron. *Irrationalzahlen*. Chelsea Publishing Company, New York, 1948.

### Saks1934

Stanisław Saks. “Remark on the differentiability of the Lebesgue indefinite
integral.” *Fundamenta Mathematicae* **22** (1934), 257–261.
