# Index: notions of the book and Rocq declarations

Source numbering and printed pages refer to
[Federer1969](bibliography.md#federer1969); see the
[bibliography](bibliography.md) for source editions.

This map follows the index of Federer, *Geometric Measure Theory* (1969),
printed pp. 672–676, in the book's order. Each row gives a notion, an item
of the book where it is defined or discussed (with the printed page), and
the declarations of the formalisation that express it: the defining
predicate or definition first, then a characterising theorem where useful.
"Not formalised" marks claims for which a corresponding declaration is
unavailable. Current completed and pending numbered items, together with
their coverage qualifications, are recorded in the section accounts linked
from [toc.md](toc.md). Each linked declaration retains its own stated
hypotheses and degree conventions.
The exterior-gauge measure T^m of 2.10.3 (pp. 171–172) is mapped by
[`is_exterior_diameter_measure`](../theories/ExteriorMeasureUP.v#L21) and [`exterior_diameter_measure`](../theories/ExteriorMeasure.v#L19); its three
covering alternatives are proved.
Notions are spelt as printed. The numbered items themselves are mapped to
files in [contents.md](contents.md); the notations of pp. 669–671 are in
[symbols.md](symbols.md).
