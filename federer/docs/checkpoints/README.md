## Reading the records

These records retain the scope and results of actual compilation,
recursive kernel checking, and assumptions audits. Read their counts,
resource limits, and paths as records of the environment at that date.
Use the [section guides](../toc.md) for current mathematical coverage.

Private progress notes, restart handoffs, and draft recovery archives
are excluded from this public snapshot. Their absence does not extend
the scope of any validated theorem.
