## Overview

Use the section guides for current mathematical coverage. The records
here state the inputs, scope and results of the checks actually performed.
A historical check does not certify later sources or unfinished drafts.
Private progress handoffs and draft recovery archives are excluded.

The [coverage completion record](coverage-completion.md) records the
completed supplements and intentional omissions. The [Ward omission
account](2.10.27-ward-omission.md) records the cited construction's scope.
