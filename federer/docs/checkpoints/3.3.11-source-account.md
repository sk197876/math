# Federer 3.3.11 source account — 4 November 2026

Reviewed 3.3.11, printed p. 297, of
[Federer1969](../bibliography.md#federer1969). The item points forward to
the example of 3.3.19 where the original product is supported on S2, and
reports that Federer knew no example with positive product mass on S3.
This is a forward reference and historical open-question account, rather
than a theorem or an assertion that such an example is impossible.
The actual 3.3.19 example has since been completed and validated.

This source account adds no proof source, assumption query, or build input.
Mathematical items 3.3.1–10 and 3.3.12–21 have separate registered
validation records. See the [section guide](../chapter3/3.3.md) and
[subsection release](3.3-validation.json).
