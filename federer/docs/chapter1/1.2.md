## Overview of the §1.2 development

All three numbered items of Federer §1.2 are covered. The development specifies
graded algebras and their direct sums (1.2.1), constructs both binary graded
tensor products (1.2.2), and constructs the tensor algebra with its tensor powers,
grouping isomorphisms, graded universal property, uniqueness and naturality
(1.2.3). The claim-by-claim inventory follows the overview.
The earlier §1.1 development remains partial.

### Interfaces

For a MathComp module `V`, a `grading V` consists of linear projections
`grade G n`, indexed by natural numbers, satisfying

\[
 P_m P_n x = \begin{cases}P_n x,&m=n,\\0,&m\ne n,\end{cases}
 \qquad
 \forall x\ \exists N\quad x=\sum_{i<N}P_i x.
\]

`homogeneous G n x` means `grade G n x = x`. Thus the components are the images
of the projections, with finite reconstruction specified as part of the data.
The closure properties and disjointness of distinct components are proved.

`graded_algebra K` adds a bilinear multiplication carrying degrees `m` and `n`
to degree `m + n`. It does not require associativity or a unit. These are
additional data in `algebra K`, used for 1.2.3. `algebra_map A B` is a bundled
linear map preserving multiplication and the unit. [`preserves_grading`](../../theories/GradedAlgebra.v#L162) states
that a linear map carries each homogeneous component into the corresponding
component. [`graded_anticommutative`](../../theories/GradedAlgebra.v#L115) states Federer's signed commutation law.
The square-zero result in degree one includes the necessary assumption
`(1 + 1 : K) != 0`.

`is_graded_tensor_algebra G iota` states Federer's graded universal property
independently of the construction. [`graded_tensor_algebra_unique`](../../theories/GradedTensorUP.v#L62) derives the
unique compatible graded algebra isomorphism from this property alone.

The stronger `is_tensor_algebra iota` is also a construction-independent proposition: every
linear map from the generating vector space into an associative unital algebra
extends uniquely to a unit-preserving algebra homomorphism. Its abstract
uniqueness theorem supplies mutually inverse algebra homomorphisms compatible
with the generators.

### The tensor algebra as a property

`is_graded_tensor_algebra iota G` (in [`TensorAlgebraUP.v`](../../theories/TensorAlgebraUP.v)) states Federer's
definition as a property of a graded associative unital algebra [`A`](../../theories/TraceAlgebra.v#L36) with a
linear map `iota : V -> A`. For every `m`, the product

\[
 (v_1,\dots,v_m)\mapsto \iota(v_1)\cdots\iota(v_m)
\]

on `m`-tuples, represented by `{dffun forall i : 'I_m, V}`, takes values of
degree `m`, and makes the degree-`m` component a tensor product of `m` copies
of `V`: every `m`-linear map `beta` corresponds to a unique linear map on [`A`](../../theories/TraceAlgebra.v#L36)
which vanishes off degree `m` and sends the product to `beta`. The empty
product is the unit.

From this property alone, [`component_span`](../../theories/TensorAlgebraUP.v#L182) and [`tensor_algebra_span`](../../theories/TensorAlgebraUP.v#L206) prove
that the products span each component and the whole algebra, and
[`graded_tensor_algebraP`](../../theories/TensorAlgebraUP.v#L427) proves `is_tensor_algebra iota`: a linear map [`f`](../../theories/ProductSpaceMaps.v#L68)
into an associative unital algebra extends uniquely to an algebra
homomorphism. The extension is assembled degree by degree from the universal
properties of the components.

[`TensorAlgebraSealed.v`](../../theories/TensorAlgebraSealed.v) provides the tensor algebra used in the sequel through
an opaquely ascribed module whose signature exposes only the algebra, the
generating map, the grading and `is_graded_tensor_algebra`. The files
[`TensorAlgebra.v`](../../theories/TensorAlgebra.v), [`TensorGrading.v`](../../theories/TensorGrading.v) and [`TensorPowers.v`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v) derive words,
products, the grading, the extension of linear maps, and the components of
degree zero and one from that property.

### Existence

Existence is proved with a construction that later files cannot see. Let `F`
be the vector space of finitely supported scalar-valued functions on `seq V`,
the set of finite words in `V`. Write `d s` for the delta function at a word
[`s`](../../theories/MultipleShuffles.v#L24). Extend

\[
 d(s)\,d(t)=d(s\mathbin{+\!+}t)
\]

bilinearly to `F`. The inductively generated subspace [`word_relation`](../../theories/WordTensorAlgebra.v#L35) contains

\[
\begin{aligned}
 d(s\mathbin{+\!+}(v+w)::t)
   &-d(s\mathbin{+\!+}v::t)-d(s\mathbin{+\!+}w::t),\\
 d(s\mathbin{+\!+}(a v)::t)
   &-a\,d(s\mathbin{+\!+}v::t)
\end{aligned}
\]

for arbitrary contexts `s,t`, vectors `v,w`, and scalars `a`; it is a
two-sided ideal. Projection onto words of length `n` descends to the quotient
and gives the grading. [`WordTensorWitness.v`](../../theories/WordTensorWitness.v) proves that the quotient
satisfies `is_graded_tensor_algebra` ([`word_tensor_algebra_exists`](../../theories/WordTensorWitness.v#L78)). The files
[`WordAlgebra.v`](../../theories/WordAlgebra.v), [`WordTensorAlgebra.v`](../../theories/WordTensorAlgebra.v), [`WordTensorGrading.v`](../../theories/WordTensorGrading.v) and
[`WordTensorPowers.v`](../../theories/WordTensorPowers.v) contain the construction.

If a target algebra has a grading, its multiplication adds degrees, its unit
is homogeneous of degree zero, and
[`f`](../../theories/ProductSpaceMaps.v#L68) takes values in degree one, the
extension preserves grading. These hypotheses are explicit in
[`graded_tensor_algebra_universal`](../../theories/TensorAlgebraMaps.v#L80).
The theorem
[`graded_tensor_algebra_exists`](../../theories/TensorPowerComponents.v#L351)
derives the unit hypothesis using
[`graded_unit_degree_zero`](../../theories/GradedUnit.v#L61), which proves.
Thus the public graded universal property has exactly Federer's target
hypotheses.

### Homogeneous components

`multilinear_words beta` expresses linearity in each position by requiring
linearity of `fun v => beta (s ++ v :: t)` for every pair of word contexts.
For a fixed degree `n`, a function on words of length `n` is represented by its
zero extension to all words. `tensor_power_universal` proves that such a
multilinear function extends uniquely to a linear map on the tensor algebra
which factors through `tensor_degree n`. This is the universal property of
the degree-`n` component in this representation; it includes degree zero.

There are explicit maps identifying the first two components:

| Component | Maps and inverse laws |
| --- | --- |
| Degree zero | [`tensor_scalar`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L345) and [`tensor_augmentation`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L259); [`tensor_scalarK`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L305) and [`tensor_zeroth_component`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L344) |
| Degree one | `tensor_generator` and [`tensor_degree_one`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L176); [`tensor_generatorK`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L194) and [`tensor_first_component`](../../theories/TensorPowers.v#L229) |

The inclusions are proved injective, and the tensor-algebra unit is proved
nonzero. No basis or finite-dimensionality assumption is used.

The actual component space is `tensor_power V n`. Its universal property is
[`tensor_power_component_universal`](../../theories/TensorPowerComponents.v#L280); the earlier whole-algebra theorem is
retained for compatibility. The scalar and degree-one identifications are also
provided as inverse linear maps between the actual component spaces.

[`tensor_power_join`](../../theories/TensorPowerProduct.v#L72) identifies the independently constructed binary tensor
product of `tensor_power V m` and `tensor_power V n` with
`tensor_power V (m+n)`. It sends a pure tensor to the product in the tensor
algebra. [`tensor_power_split`](../../theories/TensorPowerProduct.v#L149) is its inverse, obtained by splitting a word
after m letters; both cancellation laws are proved. These maps include empty
blocks. [`tensor_power_map`](../../theories/TensorPowerComponents.v#L304) applies a given linear map to each factor, and
[`tensor_algebra_map_direct_sum`](../../theories/TensorPowerComponents.v#L334) proves that these are the components of the
whole induced algebra map.

### Completion of 1.2.2

For gradings with projections `P_p` and `Q_q`, the tensor product has projections

\[
 R_n=\sum_{p=0}^{n} P_p\otimes Q_{n-p}.
\]

[`tensor_grade_orthogonal`](../../theories/GradedTensor.v#L160) and [`tensor_grade_finite`](../../theories/GradedTensor.v#L155) prove orthogonality and
finite reconstruction. [`tensor_component_induction`](../../theories/GradedTensor.v#L198) proves that the component
of degree `n` is spanned by simple tensors of homogeneous factors whose degrees
sum to `n`. This represents Federer's internal direct-sum decomposition.

`graded_tensor A B s` uses `s = false` for the ordinary multiplication and
`s = true` for the signed multiplication. The underlying space and grading are
the same in both cases. The factors are arbitrary [`graded_algebra`](../../theories/GradedAlgebra.v#L144) structures;
neither associativity nor a unit is required for the construction. The code
extends the homogeneous formulas to arbitrary elements, proves bilinearity,
and proves that the resulting multiplications are uniquely determined.

The powers are actual homogeneous component spaces of the quotient tensor
algebra. The multilinear universal property and the grouping isomorphisms
identify them with the iterated equal-factor tensor products in the book.
The inverse grouping map splits a word after m letters, and its proof includes
m=0 and n=0. All results hold in arbitrary dimension and every characteristic.
The graded uniqueness theorem uses only the stated graded universal property;
it does not assume the stronger ungraded property as a substitute.
