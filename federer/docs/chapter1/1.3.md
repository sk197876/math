[`tests/ExteriorExamples.v`](../../tests/ExteriorExamples.v) separates the product of two independent generators
from zero by an alternating coordinate functional. The functional is obtained
from the universal property of the degree-two component (through
[`exterior_word_extension`](../../theories/ExteriorGrading.v#L275)); the proof does not assume an exterior basis
theorem.

Later results use only [`is_exterior_power_algebra`](../../theories/ExteriorAlgebraUP.v#L77): for each m, the product
ι(v_1) ⋯ ι(v_m) has degree m, and the degree-m component has the universal
property for alternating m-linear maps. The quotient construction in
[`ExteriorQuotient.v`](../../theories/ExteriorQuotient.v) and [`ExteriorQuotientGrading.v`](../../theories/ExteriorQuotientGrading.v) is sealed and serves only
[`ExteriorQuotientWitness.v`](../../theories/ExteriorQuotientWitness.v).

The tensor-product basis theorem needed in the argument is proved separately
as [`tensor_basisP`](../../theories/TensorBasis.v#L118) in [`TensorBasis.v`](../../theories/TensorBasis.v), for arbitrary index sets. [`is_basis`](../../theories/LinearBasis.v#L40) in
[`LinearBasis.v`](../../theories/LinearBasis.v) means unique finite coordinates; [`basisP`](../../theories/LinearBasis.v#L181) proves its equivalence
to independence and spanning. Thus neither the tensor nor the exterior basis
theorem is assumed. [`component_basisP`](../../theories/GradedBasis.v#L160) in [`GradedBasis.v`](../../theories/GradedBasis.v) extracts an actual
homogeneous-component basis from a graded basis.

The basis theorem covers both the finite case and the countable sequence
explicitly written in Federer. Direct sums and the exterior algebra itself
remain unrestricted in dimension.

The identification of the exterior algebra of a line with two copies of the
field is an identification of graded vector spaces, with the two summands
in degrees zero and one. Its multiplication is the exterior multiplication.

The definition of [`det`](../../theories/ExteriorDeterminant.v#L139) uses the top exterior power, preceding the
coefficient calculation. [`OrderedIndices.v`](../../theories/OrderedIndices.v) and [`ExteriorPermutation.v`](../../theories/ExteriorPermutation.v)
justify the latter's sign by moving generators into order one adjacent
interchange at a time. This uses the inversion count of 1.3.3.
The coefficient family `a` is a function of two indices. No matrix
determinant theorem is used in these proofs.

MathComp composes permutations as right actions: `sigma * tau` applies
sigma first and tau second. [`phi_comp`](../../theories/PermutationDeterminant.v#L51) therefore explicitly gives
`phi (sigma * tau) x = phi tau (phi sigma x)`. The determinant composition
formula uses the usual function order [`g`](../../theories/ProductSpaceMaps.v#L53) composed with [`f`](../../theories/ProductSpaceMaps.v#L68). The scalar
index is multiplicative with either convention because the field is
commutative. No positive-dimension hypothesis is used.
