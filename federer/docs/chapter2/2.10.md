## 2.10.1 — Metric construction and the open-cover characterisation

Federer, pp. 169–171. The metric construction is formalised for arbitrary
metric spaces, indexed generator families and nonnegative extended costs.
Its definitions and standing laws were developed as prerequisites for
the earlier Hausdorff-length example. The remaining assertions, examples,
alternative formula and topological correction are now proved.

[`is_metric_scale_envelope`](../../theories/MetricScaleEnvelopeUP.v#L19) and [`is_caratheodory_limit`](../../theories/CaratheodoryLimitUP.v#L20) specify the covering
infima and their supremum before existence. [`MetricScaleEnvelopeSealed.v`](../../theories/MetricScaleEnvelopeSealed.v)
and [`CaratheodoryLimitSealed.v`](../../theories/CaratheodoryLimitSealed.v) hide the witnesses.
[`metric_scale_envelope`](../../theories/MetricScaleEnvelope.v#L16) and [`caratheodory_measure`](../../theories/CaratheodoryMeasure.v#L21) expose only those
laws. The approximations and final object satisfy Federer's measure
laws. Smaller cutoffs increase the infimum; a cutoff below the separation
of two targets gives the printed additive lower bound. All open sets
are measurable for the final measure, and Borel generators give Borel
hulls and Borel regularity. None of these statements adds separability
or finite total mass.

[`CaratheodoryScaleConvergence.v`](../../theories/CaratheodoryScaleConvergence.v) proves the full positive-cutoff
order-topology limit, with both eventual inequalities at every sufficiently
small positive cutoff. This includes an infinite final value and is
stronger than recording convergence only along reciprocal cutoffs.
[`CaratheodorySingleGenerator.v`](../../theories/CaratheodorySingleGenerator.v) computes exact masses for a single
generator. [`CaratheodoryApproximationCounterexamples.v`](../../theories/CaratheodoryApproximationCounterexamples.v) supplies the
two printed observations explicitly: with only [0,1] as generator, of
cost one, its cutoff-one approximation assigns mass one to every
nonempty subset of the interval. The open half-line below one half
fails the Caratheodory splitting identity. The final mass of that
same generator is infinity, since it cannot cover any nonempty target
below its diameter; the final measure therefore does not extend the cost.

[`metric_caratheodory_open_cover_characterization`](../../theories/MetricRefinementCharacterization.v#L66) proves the complete
alternative formula on p. 170: the metric measure of A is the infimum
of thresholds t for which every open cover of A has a countable generator
refinement covering A with total cost strictly below t.
[`MetricScaleApproximation.v`](../../theories/MetricScaleApproximation.v) derives cover choices from the infimum laws.
[`MetricOpenCoverLayers.v`](../../theories/MetricOpenCoverLayers.v) exhausts A by open layers with a uniform
admissible mesh on each layer. Disjointifying those layers gives a
measurable partition. Countable additivity after restriction applies
even when A is not measurable. [`MetricRefinementApproximation.v`](../../theories/MetricRefinementApproximation.v)
covers each part with a summable error, discards generators not meeting
that part, and unites the remaining families. Their total cost is at
most the measure of A plus half the requested error. Uniform open-ball
covers give the reverse bound in [`MetricRefinementCharacterization.v`](../../theories/MetricRefinementCharacterization.v).
This proof replaces the corrected source paper's countable subcover and
two-index layers by reciprocal-ball layers; no new hypothesis is added.

There is a **local correction to the topological remark** on p. 170.
Taking its printed quantifier over open covers only of A literally in
an arbitrary topological space can fail monotonicity.
[`printed_topological_refinement_not_measure`](../../theories/OpenRefinementNonmonotonicity.v#L90) uses a sealed two-point
space whose open sets are empty, {q}, and the whole space {p,q}.
Take the whole space as the sole generator, of cost one. For A={q}, the
open cover {{q}} has no generator refinement, so the threshold infimum
is infinity. Every open cover of the whole space contains the whole
space itself, so its infimum is one. A subset thus has greater value
than the whole space. The topology is specified by
[`is_two_point_open_topology`](../../theories/TwoPointOpenTopologyUP.v#L20), constructed privately and sealed in
[`TwoPointOpenTopologySealed.v`](../../theories/TwoPointOpenTopologySealed.v); the counterexample uses only its laws.

One valid arbitrary-topological extension quantifies over open covers
of the **whole space**. [`OpenRefiningEnvelopeUP.v`](../../theories/OpenRefiningEnvelopeUP.v) specifies the infimum
for a fixed cover. [`OpenRefiningEnvelopeConstruction.v`](../../theories/OpenRefiningEnvelopeConstruction.v) proves existence
using the existing countable-cover envelope and a proved infinite-cost
encoding for forbidden generators; its selected witness is sealed.
[`TopologicalCaratheodoryUP.v`](../../theories/TopologicalCaratheodoryUP.v) specifies the supremum over whole-space
open covers. Its existence proof and [`TopologicalCaratheodorySealed.v`](../../theories/TopologicalCaratheodorySealed.v)
precede the public [`topological_caratheodory_measure`](../../theories/TopologicalCaratheodory.v#L20).

[`topological_caratheodory_is_measure`](../../theories/TopologicalCaratheodoryMeasure.v#L23) proves Federer's measure laws for
this corrected construction. [`topological_caratheodory_refinement_characterization`](../../theories/TopologicalOpenRefinementBound.v#L72)
identifies it with the corresponding strict-threshold infimum.
[`topological_caratheodory_continuous_measurable`](../../theories/TopologicalContinuousMeasurability.v#L29) proves the stated
measurability of every continuous map into an arbitrary metric target.
Common open-cover refinements retain two independent cover constraints;
a pulled-back small-ball cover prevents any generator from meeting both
separated image pieces. Split the generator indices, take both suprema,
and apply the already proved criterion 2.3.2(8).
[`topological_caratheodory_metric_agreement`](../../theories/TopologicalMetricAgreement.v#L30) proves equality with the
original construction on every metric space. The corrected quantifier
therefore preserves the printed metric result. No separation axiom
is added to the general source topology, and no Borel-generator
hypothesis is used for this continuous-map conclusion.

### Local correction: the family of closed balls need not be closed

The p. 188 assertion about closed balls in the Hausdorff metric
is false even for a compact real subspace. Put

$$C=[-1,-1/2]\cup[1/2,1],\qquad X=C\cup\{-2,2\}.$$

Use the real distance restricted to X. A centre in the positive
component needs radius at least a+1 to include -1, which also
includes 2; a negative centre similarly includes -2. An outer
centre includes itself. Hence C is not a closed ball in X.
For $0<h\le1/8$, the relative closed ball with centre $1/2+h$
and radius $3/2-2h$ is

$$B_h=[-1+3h,-1/2]\cup[1/2,1].$$

It lies in C and its Hausdorff distance from C is at most 3h.
[`closed_ball_hausdorff_closedness_counterexample`](../../theories/ClosedBallHausdorffApproximation.v#L129) proves the
existence of an actual compact representative of C that is not
a relative ball and is approached arbitrarily closely by actual
positive-radius relative balls. [`split_ball_space_compact`](../../theories/ClosedBallHausdorffCounterexample.v#L62)
checks compactness of X. The Hausdorff metric is represented
through the isometric inclusion of the real subspace in R;
its restricted distances are unchanged. The counterexample
refutes this auxiliary justification. The spherical
weighted-integral equality is proved by the comparison route below.

This is a local correction, distinct from Federer's erratum.
The erratum, p. 4, book p. 188 line 21, corrects only the script
S notation. The general equality question on p. 187 is retained
as a historical remark, not introduced as a project axiom or
claimed to be settled by the conditional results.
